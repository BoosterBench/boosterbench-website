Features

Everything in one app

Five simple tabs for everyone: Home, Teams, Chat, Volunteer and Give. What you can do depends on your role, and your organization decides who gets which powers. Organizers also get a desktop website for setup and reports.

Families Coaches Booster clubs Fundraising and payments Schools Youth programs

For families

What parents and students see

Free for every family. One login covers every child, every team and every organization they belong to.

Home: what's coming up

The next three days of games and practices for all your kids in one list, marked by child. A countdown to the next big home game, and cards for anything waiting on you.

Games and practices

Games come straight from Schedule Galaxy (Aktivate). Coaches add practices. Any change or cancellation sends an alert, with a map link and Add to my calendar.

Secure team chat

Team + Parents and Parents-only chats for every team. Message a coach without anyone sharing a cell number. Quiet hours 10 PM to 5 AM.

Volunteer in two taps

Claim a snack bar shift or pledge food items. Reminders the day before and two hours before. Can't make it? The spot reopens for someone else.

Hours and points

Check in and out with one button. Hours, community service hours and points add up for each parent, each child and the family.

News feed and alerts

Posts from the school, club and coaches. Save posts, hide pinned ones, and choose which alerts you get.

"I'm going"

RSVP to games and events, see the crowd count grow, and invite friends to pack the stands on Hype Nights.

Team photo albums

Private albums for each team. A parent OKs any photo their child posts, and every photo gets an automatic check.

Private carpool

Ask or offer rides for any game or practice, only to carpool friends you choose. Meeting spots, never home addresses.

Fan clubs

Anyone in the school can join a team's fan club to follow its schedule, news and photos, without joining the team chat.

Feedback and surveys

Send a respectful note to a coach or the club, with your name or anonymously. Answer surveys anonymously anytime.

Search

Find any game, event, sign-up, team or message in your own chats.

For coaches

Run your team from your phone

  • Secure team group chats with players and parents, plus one-on-one chats with a parent, or with a player (their parent is always copied). Nobody hands out a personal cell number.
  • Announcements to the whole team, just parents, or just players, with an optional phone alert
  • Practice schedule: add practices with a weekly repeat, right next to your games, and changes alert families automatically
  • Create events like team dinners, senior nights and car washes, with RSVPs, help sign-ups and flyers
  • Team fundraising: start a fundraiser for your team, approved by your club or AD, and see who gave and the running total
  • Urgent override for a cancellation or bus delay during quiet hours
  • Score reminder after each game, and an AI recap draft you edit and approve
  • Quick polls, volunteer shifts and hours for your own team

For booster clubs

Run the club, not the paperwork

Club roles match how boards really work: a president with every power, a treasurer who sees the money (and no chats), and a volunteer coordinator for sign-ups and hours.

Leader dashboard

Three big numbers (volunteer hours, money raised per event, money raised overall) and a "Needs your attention" list. Download any report as a spreadsheet.

Shifts for a whole season

Post concession shifts and food lists for every home game at once. See which shifts are still empty.

Confirm who helped

After each event, confirm hours with a tap. Badges at 10, 25 and 50 hours, and printable service hours records for students.

Points and rewards

Set your own rules: what earns points, what they buy (hoodies, clinics, a family requirement), and when they reset. Spirit points and a House Cup if your school wants one.

Events and Hype Nights

One event builder for tournaments, banquets, alumni games and Pink Out nights. "Hype me up" suggests themes, posters and an alert plan.

Requests to teams

Ask teams to send helpers ("4 players for Trunk or Treat"). Coaches answer in one tap, and a scoreboard shows team totals.

Snack bar status

Every home game shows if the snack bar is open and tonight's hot food menu.

Local sponsor cards

Sell sponsorships with your own levels, like Gold $500 and Silver $250. Sponsors get a simple report. Your club keeps 75%.

Surveys

Interest checks, end-of-season surveys and quick polls, with live charts and Excel export.

Fundraising and payments

Every dollar, tracked

Money goes straight to your organization's own bank account through Stripe. Families pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Cash App Pay. Cash is always welcome too.

Donations and fundraisers

  • Give to the club, a team, or a fundraiser with a progress bar ("New scoreboard: $3,200 of $5,000")
  • One-time or monthly gifts. Grandparents and alumni can give from a web link with no app
  • Emailed receipts, marked tax-deductible when your club has IRS status
  • "Cover the card fee" is on by default, and donors can round up for the club
  • Donor names stay private unless the donor chooses to be thanked publicly
  • Coaches can start team fundraisers, approved by a club leader or AD

Event tickets

  • Sell tickets for banquets, tournaments and alumni games, or give out free tickets
  • Tickets arrive in the app as a QR code, and a volunteer scans them at the door
  • No signal? Download the ticket list before the event, or an Excel list of everyone who paid
  • Parents tap-sign tournament waivers for each player under 18
  • Sales tax on or off, with your own rate

Snack bar payments

  • The app makes a QR code for each event's stand. Print it or show it on a phone
  • Fans scan it, type the total the volunteer tells them, and show the green "Paid" screen
  • Works even when the stand has no internet, as long as the buyer's phone has signal
  • Volunteers tap in cash sales so the night's totals stay right
  • Students can pay too

Spirit wear store

  • One athletics store with apparel for every sport, inside the Give tab
  • Print-on-demand, so your club never buys or stores inventory
  • Home shipping or school pickup, with optional order windows before playoffs
  • Your club sets its own profit and sees every order
  • Students can buy with a parent's OK

Youth sign-up fees

  • Parents pay when they register, with sibling and early-bird discounts applied automatically
  • Payment plans for fees over $25
  • Private fee-help requests that only the director sees
  • Directors can mark cash payments

Easy money setup

  • One authorized person (treasurer, president or owner) fills in the tax paperwork once
  • Connect the bank account through Stripe. BoosterBench never sees or stores card or bank numbers
  • The treasurer sees all money reports, and big changes need the owner's OK
  • For school booster clubs, the AD gets a notice when money collection turns on

For schools and athletic directors

One system for every team

You approve who's in

Add, verify and remove coaches and club leaders. Families join only with your invite codes. Remove anyone instantly.

Reported messages come to you

You see every safety report. Messages can't be secretly deleted.

School-wide and emergency alerts

Reach every family at once, including emergency alerts during quiet hours.

Your colors and logo

Upload your mascot and pick your colors, with a full preview before you save.

Feedback and safety concerns

Safety concerns like bullying, hazing or injury skip the coach and come straight to you.

End-of-season surveys

A reminder when it's time, and you tap Send. You choose what coaches see.

Service hours rules

Choose which events count for community service, and name an approver like the honor society advisor.

Post approval switch

Turn on approval before anything goes out school-wide.

Privacy paperwork ready

We sign your district's data privacy agreement and follow FERPA, COPPA and NY Ed Law 2-d.

For town rec, Little League and travel teams

Youth sign-ups without the paperwork pile

Independent programs get their own BoosterBench account. No school needed.

  • Parents register each child in three steps: pick the child, answer questions and sign forms, then pay
  • Live totals, shirt counts, and a list of who hasn't paid
  • Download rosters, shirt orders and emergency sheets
  • Teams stay private until you tap "Announce teams," then every family is told at once
  • Coaches can call a player's emergency contact from the roster without seeing the number
  • Optional grant questions, shown to directors as totals only
  • One family login across school and town teams, with walls between organizations
