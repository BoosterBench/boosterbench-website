Your account

Delete your BoosterBench account

You can delete your account any time. It can't be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open BoosterBench and tap your picture to open My profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account. It happens right away.

If you're the only president of a club that has other members, the app asks you to make another leader president first, so the club isn't left without one.

Without the app

Email hello@boosterbench.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, name and email
  • Your roles at every school and club
  • Your family list, and children's sign-up details you entered
  • Your volunteer sign-ups, RSVPs, carpool settings, saved posts and alerts

What stays

Messages and posts you wrote stay for the team, shown as "Former member" instead of your name. A student's own account isn't deleted, but it is no longer linked to you. Payment receipts that the law requires a club to keep stay with the club.
