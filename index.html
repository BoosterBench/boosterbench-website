What's inside

Built around game day

Volunteers who show up, and hours that count themselves

Post snack bar shifts for any home game in a minute. Families sign up from their phones and get a reminder the day before.

  • Families sign up to bring food items, not just work shifts
  • Volunteers check in and out at the stand, so hours are logged for you
  • Separate totals for community service hours
  • Friendly team-by-team totals that never name who didn't help

Coach chat with parents always included

Every team gets a Team + Parents chat and a Parents-only chat. Coaches, players and families talk without anyone giving out a cell number.

  • Quiet hours from 10 PM to 5 AM for everyone
  • No adult alone in a chat with a student, ever
  • Cell numbers and emails stay private unless a parent chooses to share
  • Press and hold any message to report it

Your schedule, not a new system

BoosterBench reads the games you already keep in Schedule Galaxy or Aktivate, and coaches add practices. Changes alert families automatically, so nobody types a game schedule twice.

  • Every child's games and practices in one list on Home
  • "I'm going" buttons help pack the stands and plan the snack bar
  • Events like banquets, pasta nights and Hype Nights

Safe by design

Student safety comes first

These rules are built into the app. Nobody has to remember to turn them on.

  • No adult alone with a studentA coach can't privately message a single student. Messages to a coach copy a parent.
  • Quiet hours, 10 PM to 5 AMFor everyone. Only urgent alerts from approved leaders go out overnight.
  • Contact details stay privatePhone numbers and emails are hidden unless a parent chooses to share.
  • Your admin is in chargeApprove coaches, remove anyone instantly, review reported messages.
  • Your data is yoursWe never sell it, and students never see ads.
  • Breach notice within 72 hoursWell inside New York's 7-day limit.
