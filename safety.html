Safety and privacy

Built to protect students

These rules run underneath every feature. No coach, club or school can turn them off, and they apply in every state.

Messaging

Safe conversations

No adult alone with a student

A coach can never privately message a single student. A student's message to a coach always copies a parent.

Quiet hours

Chat and alerts pause from 10 PM to 5 AM for everyone. Only emergencies, urgent cancellations and rides already set go through.

Private contact details

Phone numbers, emails and home addresses stay hidden unless a parent chooses to share their own with their team's parents. Students can never share theirs.

Report and block everywhere

On every message, post and photo. Reports go to club leaders and the school admin.

Nothing secretly deleted

Messages are saved, so problems can be looked into.

Kindness filter

Blocked words are caught before sending. The app shows what was flagged and asks the person to say it another way.

Your data

Your families' data stays yours

  • We never sell personal informationAnd we never build profiles of students.
  • No ads to studentsSponsor cards show only to adults, and never in chat.
  • No accounts under 13Younger kids take part through a parent's account. Every student 18 and under is linked to a parent or guardian.
  • Never collectedNo Social Security numbers, birth certificate copies or street addresses.
  • Every organization is walled offPeople see only their own organization's teams, chats and people.
  • Breach notice within 72 hoursWell inside New York's 7-day limit.
