Pricing questions

Do families pay anything?

No. Parents, students, coaches and supporters join free. They only pay for things they choose to buy or give.

Does our booster club need its own plan if the school has one?

Each organization buys its own plan. Talk to us about how your school and club are set up, and we'll find the simplest fit.

Will families see ads?

Adults may see your local sponsors' cards on Home, the Give tab and event pages. Ads never appear in chats, and students never see any ads. Later, adults may also see a few national brand ads, and your organization can pay to turn those off.

Who holds the money?

Your organization does. Payments go to your own Stripe-connected bank account. BoosterBench never stores card or bank numbers.
